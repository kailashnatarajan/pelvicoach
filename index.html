Count only the squeezes that count.

PelviCoach is a pelvic floor trainer for older women with urinary incontinence. It checks the sensor signal, watches for belly-muscle cheating, and credits a repetition only when it was done correctly. Clinicians review the results and set the targets.

How every repetition is judged

The checks run in this order, and the first one that fails decides the result. The reason is always shown to the patient.

  1. 1
    MeasureTwo sensors read pelvic floor activity and abdominal activity, as a share of the patient's own maximum.
  2. 2
    Check the signalA flat line (loose electrode) or 50 Hz mains interference makes the rep INVALID. It is not scored and never changes the plan.
  3. 3
    Look for compensationIf the abdomen is doing the work, the rep is COMPENSATED and the patient gets a cue to relax.
  4. 4
    Compare with the targetBelow the target for the whole hold is a MISS. Otherwise the rep is OK and counts toward the goal.
  5. 5
    Adapt the trainingThree OK reps in a row make the next target harder. Two misses in a row make it easier. The rule is simple and visible to everyone.
  6. 6
    Store and reviewEach result is saved with its reason, so the clinician can see what happened and change targets, hold time and reps.

One platform, two views

Patients and clinicians sign in separately and see different things.

For patients

  • Guided exercises with a short video for each one
  • Live feedback: pelvic activity, abdominal activity, signal quality, target and hold timer
  • A clear result and reason after every repetition
  • Past sessions and progress in one place

For clinicians

  • See only the patients who have shared their link code
  • Results by type: OK, missed, compensated, invalid
  • Trends over time for success rate, activity and targets
  • Adjust target intensity, hold time and repetitions

Privacy and security

Sign-in is required. A patient can only open their own records. A clinician can only open patients who gave them a link code, and the patient can remove that link at any time. Only repetition results are stored, not raw sensor signals.

PelviCoach is a prototype rehabilitation and biofeedback platform developed for demonstration and research purposes. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic or treatment device. Clinical use requires appropriate validation and qualified healthcare supervision.
